Years in Enterprise IT & Web

I'm Ameer Ridzuan (Juan), a System Engineer and MBOT Graduate Technologist (GR.TECH) at Kossan Rubber Industries Berhad. I specialise in enterprise application support, relational database management, and full-stack workflow automation—turning manual Excel processes into scalable internal web systems with multi-role RBAC.

My day-to-day work covers modernising legacy platforms (Delphi 5 to Delphi 12/13 & Firebird 2.5 to 5), supporting production-critical ERP/WMS environments (KPCSv1/v2, SAP Business One, MS D365), and building in-house platforms such as our Internal PMO Web Portal and CNI Server Room Management System.

Previously, I interned at HeiTech Padu Berhad as a UI/UX & System Designer, crafting interfaces and front-end modules for the HeiDi digital platform and JPJ Portal. Outside enterprise work, I build independent digital products—including by.ridzu.one (digital wedding invitation cards), GymFlow, and SplitSukan.

Core Technical Focus
• Web & App Engineering: PHP, JavaScript, Next.js/PWA, .NET, Delphi, RESTful APIs & RBAC.
• Database & Enterprise: MS SQL Server (SSMS), MySQL, Firebird, SAP B1, SIT/UAT & CI/CD.
• UI/UX & Creative: Figma, Design-to-Code, Adobe Lightroom, Photoshop & Premiere Pro.
